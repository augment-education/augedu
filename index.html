How a session works

Question. Explore. Debate. Create.

01

Ask questions

What do you find interesting about this subject? How can you find more information? Why does it matter to you?

02

Find answers

Does the information make sense? How many new questions pop up along the way?

03

Discuss and share

Classmates take turns debating and defending different opinions. Communication is built into every subject.

04

Collaborate and create

Students grow a personal digital asset library and visual knowledge trees, a portfolio of real work.

Lessons follow a structured “I Do, We Do, You Do” protocol, with First Principles cheat sheets so any adult can lead.

Four pillars

Built on first principles.

Inquiry-based

Stoke curiosity and critical thinking. Students answer their own questions through demonstrable assignments and First Principles learning.

Individualized

Ownership and fulfillment. Multiple pathways for diverse learners, each moving at their own pace with teacher guidance.

Portfolio-building

A body of work that builds year after year. Students own it in their school or personal Google, Canva, M365 or other platform.

Pathway

Aligned with national standards: ISTE, CSTA and state frameworks, with cross-curricular value in ELA, STEM and digital production.
