What students explore

From the first fire to the age of AI

Learning the history of technology gives students a deeper view of the world. By following how tools evolved, they see the ingenuity and perseverance behind the devices we take for granted today.

Historical context also helps students judge technology’s impact on society, from how people connect to how economies and cultures change. And because technology is never static, it builds a growth mindset: always look for a better way.
