What students explore

Safe, thoughtful, capable technology use

Digital citizenship is the starting point for everything else. Students explore how to treat people well online, protect their accounts and personal information, and think critically about what they see.

Along the way they build a digital footprint they can be proud of, and practice speaking up for others.
